Who We Are

Born From a
Passion for Beauty

Lumière was born in the cobblestone studios of Paris in 2010, when founder Elise Fontaine — a third-generation goldsmith — decided the world needed jewelry that felt as extraordinary as the women who would wear it.

Today, our collection spans over 248 unique handcrafted pieces, each telling a story of artistry, intention, and timeless elegance. We believe that fine jewelry is not a luxury reserved for the few — it is a right of every woman who dares to feel extraordinary.

Every Lumière piece passes through the hands of at least five master artisans before it reaches yours. We source only ethically certified gemstones and recycled metals, because beauty should never come at someone else's expense.

What Drives Us

Our Values

01

Artisanal Craftsmanship

Every piece is hand-finished by master jewelers with decades of experience. No shortcuts, no mass production — only genuine artistry.

02

Ethical Sourcing

We are committed to 100% conflict-free gemstones and recycled precious metals. Our planet matters as much as your jewelry.

03

Timeless Design

We design pieces that transcend trends. Lumière jewelry is meant to be worn for a lifetime — and passed down as heirloom.

04

Woman-First Philosophy

We design for real women — every shape, every occasion, every story. Our collections celebrate the full spectrum of femininity.

How It's Made

Our Process

01

Design & Sketch

Every piece begins as a hand-drawn sketch inspired by nature, architecture, and feminine form.

02

Stone Selection

Our gemologist hand-picks each stone for cut, clarity, and character. Only the finest make the cut.

03

Handcrafting

Master artisans shape, set, and polish each piece by hand — a process that takes days, not minutes.

04

Quality Assurance

Five rounds of quality inspection before packaging in our signature luxury box, ready for you.
